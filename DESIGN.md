## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-momentum, conversion-engineered e-commerce experience. Built on a foundation of high-contrast contemporary minimalism paired with kinetic, purposeful color distribution, it blends utilitarian precision with consumer urgency.

### Core Character
- **Dynamic Vitality:** The interface feels alive and reactive, deploying concentrated bursts of energetic flame orange against deep obsidian and optic white planes to create immediate optical paths to purchase.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts rely on strict typographic rhythm, generous macro-whitespace, and dense, structured micro-information modules that respect user cognitive load while maximizing product desirability.
- **Tactile Modernity:** Clean surface shifts, crisp pill-form interaction anchors, and subtly tinted atmospheric elevations replace heavy visual noise, evoking direct, premium physical goods.

## Colors

The palette operates on a high-tension triad: luminous kinetic orange, impenetrable obsidian black, and graded neutral substrates. Color is never decorative; it is strictly functional and directional.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#FF5400` / `#FF6B00`):** Reserved exclusively for high-intent interactive drivers: Primary Conversion Actions (Add to Cart, Checkout, Buy Now), flash-sale flags, critical micro-states (active selections, focus outlines), and key metrics.
- **Secondary / Deep Obsidian (`#0A0A0A` / `#121212`):** Defines core structural frames, persistent sticky navigation shells, high-contrast banner surfaces, and foundational display typography.
- **Tertiary Tint (`#FF8533`):** Utilized for interactive hover shifts, soft glow effects, and secondary promotional alerts.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:**
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FFFFFF` for primary product displays and clean viewports.
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F8F9FA` for alternating section backdrops and inactive pill containers.
  - `surface-muted`: `#F3F4F6` for card enclosures, subtle divider lines, and disabled modules.
- **Text & Contrast Hierarchy:**
  - `text-primary`: `#0A0A0A` (16.8:1 contrast against pure white).
  - `text-secondary`: `#52525B` (WCAG AAA compliant for critical body specifications).
  - `text-muted`: `#A1A1AA` for metadata, de-emphasized SKUs, and empty states.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial-grade hierarchy balanced by pure utilitarian readability. 

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Tightly tracked with heavy weights (700/800) to anchor product value propositions and campaign headers with decisive commercial weight.
- **Body Text (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse type used for item descriptions, reviews, checkout flows, and technical specifications.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Small caps, badges, and CTA labels use positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) and medium/bold weights to guarantee instant comprehension at glance speed.
- **Price Presentation:** Currency symbols render at standard weight, while integer amounts display bold and compact to enhance visual perceived value.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptable 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded by a max structural container of `1360px` to maintain optimal scan lines on ultra-wide viewports.

### Breakpoints & Responsive Columns
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin safe-zones set to `16px` (`space-md`), gutters at `12px`. Product listings reflow to a 2-column compact grid or 1-column high-detail stack.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margins set to `24px` (`gutter-tablet`), gutters at `16px`. E-commerce category filters compress into collapsible horizontal scrolling pill rails or a sliding drawer.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout. Margins set to `32px` (`gutter-desktop`), gutters at `24px` to `32px`. Sticky checkout summaries and dynamic facet filtering lock into persistent rails.

### Spacing Principles
All spacing adheres strictly to an 8px base rhythm (with a 4px half-step for micro-alignments like badge padding and icon-to-text gaps). Compact internal padding keeps e-commerce listing units information-dense, while structural section breaks (`space-3xl` and `space-4xl`) provide editorial breathing room between merchandised modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered structural tones reinforced by ultra-subtle, warm-tinted ambient shadows, rejecting harsh artificial drop shadows in favor of a polished, physical studio quality.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F9FA` base canvases. Zero shadow. Used for category list containers and plain narrative sections.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards):** Border stroke `1px solid rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.06)` combined with an ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.02)`. Gives product surfaces tactile lift without dominating the frame.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Cards):** Border stroke shifts to `1px solid rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.12)`. Shadow escalates to `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.04)`. Card subtly translates `-2px` on the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Floating Overlays / Sticky Elements):** Sticky headers, quick-cart flyouts, and dropdown pickers use `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.12)`. Headers employ `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with a `16px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) and a `1px solid rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)` bottom divider.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Sheet Drawers):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.24)`. Backdrops receive an obsidian tint: `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.6)` with `4px` blur to focus complete visual attention on transactional flows.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a refined rounded aesthetic (`roundedness: 2`) that balances consumer friendliness with razor-sharp geometric precision.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 8px / 0.5rem):** Form inputs, dropdown selectors, internal media thumbnails, and notification toasts.
- **Container Enclosures (`rounded-lg`, 12px to 16px / 0.75rem - 1rem):** Primary product cards, merchandising modules, promotion cards, and cart sheets.
- **Floating Containers (`rounded-xl`, 20px to 24px / 1.25rem - 1.5rem):** Hero highlight containers, full modal dialogs, and slide-in trays.
- **Pill-Form Elements (Fully Rounded, 9999px):** All actionable conversion buttons, category chips, discount badges, inventory status indicators, and floating back-to-top anchors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion Button (Add to Cart / Buy Now):**
  - Geometry: Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), height `48px` (desktop: `52px`).
  - Styling: Solid background `#FF5400`, text `#FFFFFF`, typography `label-lg`.
  - Hover / Focus: Background shifts to `#E64A00` with an active micro-scale press effect (`scale(0.98)`). Focus ring: `3px solid rgba(255, 84, 0, 0.35)` with a `2px` white offset.
- **Secondary Transactional (Obsidian):**
  - Styling: Solid background `#0A0A0A`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used for checkout progression and high-contrast filters. Hover shifts to `#262626`.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:**
  - Styling: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E4E4E7`, text `#0A0A0A`. Hover fills with `#F8F9FA` and darkens border to `#0A0A0A`.

### Product Cards
- **Architecture:** White surface bounded by `1px solid rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.06)`, `16px` border-radius (`rounded-lg`), hidden overflow.
- **Image Container:** 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio with `#F8F9FA` background. Floating badges positioned at top-left/top-right margins (`8px` offset).
- **Interactive Quick-Add:** A secondary micro-pill button anchored at bottom-right or revealed on desktop card hover with a rapid fade/slide upward transition (`duration: 180ms ease-out`).
- **Data Block:** Padding `16px`. Hierarchy follows: Micro-category/vendor (`label-sm`, `#71717A`), Title (`title-md`, `#0A0A0A`), Price block (`price-display`, `#0A0A0A` with optional crossed-out comparison in `#A1A1AA`).

### Badge Tags & Status Chips
- **Discount & Sale Badges:** Pill shape, padding `4px 10px`, background `#FF5400`, text `#FFFFFF`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Category & Filter Chips:** Background `#F3F4F6`, border `1px solid transparent`, text `#0A0A0A`. When selected: Background `#0A0A0A`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Inventory Badges:** Low stock alerts use a muted background `rgba(255, 84, 0, 0.1)` with solid `#FF5400` text and a pulsing micro-dot.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Container:** Height `48px`, border-radius `8px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D4D4D8`. Typography `body-md`.
- **State Changes:** Hover shifts border to `#A1A1AA`. Focused state features a crisp `#0A0A0A` border with a subtle `2px` ambient halo. Invalid entries swap border to vibrant crimson with clear inline helper text.
- **Sticky Search Navigation:** Features integrated instant-clear actions, recent search tag popovers, and immediate thumbnail autocomplete dropdowns.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** `20px x 20px`, `4px` border radius. Inactive: border `1.5px solid #D4D4D8`. Checked: solid `#0A0A0A` background with crisp white icon checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons (Variant Selectors):** Swatches utilize a double-ring mechanism. Outermost ring highlights selection in `#0A0A0A` with a `2px` white space gap around the inner color token.

### Sticky Navigation Shell
- **Placement & Dimensions:** Top-locked, fixed height `68px` (mobile: `60px`).
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` at `90%` opacity paired with `16px` blur filter.
- **Cart Trigger:** Features a live animated badge counter in energetic orange (`#FF5400`) that triggers a subtle scale bounce upon item insertion.
